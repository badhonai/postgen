Every public chain is a glass ledger. Your balance, your counterparty, your timing. All of it readable, forever. Fluton keeps a different book: it holds no balances, only notes. Each note is one sealed commitment, a leaf in a single append-only tree. Spend it and it becomes three, and value is conserved: what goes in, plus what crosses the boundary, equals what comes out. All the chain learns is a nullifier, proof that a note is spent, never which one. Recipient, amount, fee and ciphertexts are bound into one hash, so nobody can retarget your transaction. Only the net amount crossing the pool is public. Deposits and withdrawals show their size. Everything inside stays sealed.
