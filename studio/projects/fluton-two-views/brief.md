# Project brief — Fluton: two views

Status: **animatic** (voice pending). The picture, the timing model and the score are finished.

- **Audience / platform:** X, 16:9 in-feed. Privacy-literate crypto followers who already know what a
  public chain shows.
- **Single message:** the chain sees a hash, a nullifier and the gas — never the amount, the
  receiver or the link. Action: read the thread under the post.
- **Mechanism (new to this film, not reused):** a vertical **witness seam** down the middle of the
  frame — everything left of it is what you see, right of it is what the chain sees — plus
  **redaction bars** that cover printed values on their spoken beats.
- **Aspect / length:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, 1780 frames (59.33 s) at the current timing model.
- **Brand:** user-supplied mark + wordmark, byte-exact, declared in `assets.json`.
- **Voice:** Liam (`TX3LPaxmHKxFdv7VOQHJ`), one take, no speed or pitch change — **not generated
  yet**; timings are currently estimated (`--estimate=2.5`) so the picture could be built and judged.
- **Music:** original synthesis from `tools/score.py` driven by `production/cues.json`
  (9 sections, 27 accents at 120 BPM, generated by `tools/cues-from-lines.py`).
- **Constraints:** no gradients, glow, blur, particles, RGB split or shake; one idea per chapter;
  the words are the animation.
