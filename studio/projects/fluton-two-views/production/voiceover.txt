You sign one payment. Two different people watch it land. You see the amount, the receiver, and the fee you paid. The chain sees a hash, a nullifier, and the gas. Nothing else comes with it. It never learns what moved, or who received it. So how does it know the payment is real? Fluton checks the numbers while they stay encrypted. Value still balances: everything that goes in comes out. Not one cent is created, and nothing is revealed. What stays public is the size of the crossing. Privacy is not the absence of proof.
