# Prepared production brief

## User-selected scope
30-second beginner-friendly energetic Fluton explainer, 16:9 / 1920×1080 / 60 fps. This run uses Claude only for visual/code making; the orchestrator owns everything else. This is an optional mode, not a permanent change to future films.

## Message
Fluton combines computation on encrypted state with proof-based private note movement on public blockchain infrastructure. Privacy has explicit boundaries. The documented pool is testnet-only; never use it with real funds.

## Concept selection by the orchestrator
1. **Public Rails. Private Actions. — selected baseline.** A visible activity trail becomes a cutaway of different privacy functions, then exposes the entry/exit boundary. It makes FHE and ZK visually distinct and teaches limits rather than promising total invisibility.
2. **Your intent. Not your data. — rejected for this new film.** Too close to the earlier shutter/engine/logo-docking film; the headline can sound absolute without boundary context.
3. **Follow a private payment — rejected as a literal UI tutorial.** A 30-second wallet walkthrough risks misleading implementation/status and needs exact live product screens. Our film stays conceptual.

## Identity / art direction
Ink #09090B, white #F8FAFC, green #05DF72, blue #2B63D9; this is the established project palette, not a claim of a full official brand manual. Inter 600/900. Use the exact supplied white wordmark/mark, not recreated lettering. Large graphic actions, varied visual silhouettes, strong contrast and readable holds. No generic particles/glow, repeated disc wipes, fake dashboard or reprise of the old extruded-mark docking finale.

Full-bleed backgrounds; no bottom black bar. Draft captions sit directly over scene colour outside the content clip. Main action must stay above y=880. A final testnet notice is supplied by the wrapper.

## Audience and output
A viewer who knows public chains exist but does not know FHE/ZK. Explain the roles through action; no cryptographic jargon wall, fabricated numbers, chain integrations, guarantees, mainnet claims or investment instruction. CTA: fluton.io / explore the testnet. Not a seamless loop.

## Audio and timing
Voice script and 30-second visual windows are planning baselines, not a recording or word-level alignment. Do not change narrator/speed/pitch or spend credits. The orchestrator will arrange the selected recording and original music after the visual return; if a performance does not fit, resolve that transparently rather than forcing it. No voice generation is authorized by merely preparing this folder.
