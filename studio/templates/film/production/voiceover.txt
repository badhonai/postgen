Write the first line here. Then a second sentence that carries the thought.

Rules for a script that edits well:
- one idea per sentence, 8 to 14 words, because one sentence is one chapter;
- 100 to 130 words total for a 45 to 60 second film;
- write the words you want to see set large on screen, not the words a narrator would pad with;
- end on the thing you want remembered, in five words or fewer.

Generate the voice, then keep the provider's alignment JSON in
production/narration.alignment.json and the audio in production/narration.mp3.
