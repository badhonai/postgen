## Current brief

## Chosen concept

A readable intent becomes concealed data; its encrypted form remains active inside an FHE + ZK engine, then resolves into Fluton's privacy-by-default identity.

Three directions considered:
1. **Your intent. Not your data. — selected.** Information concealment is the actual transformation mechanism; the engine and exact brand mark carry the payoff. Specific to Fluton rather than a recolored design reel.
2. Cross-chain route map — rejected for this short cut: too many ecosystem marks, risks implying live integrations without explaining deployment status.
3. Developer SDK launch — rejected: the homepage still calls the intent SDK coming soon; a fake interface or undocumented API would be misleading.
