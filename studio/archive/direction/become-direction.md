## Film in one sentence

A word is taken apart so its pieces can build something with depth.

## Direction

- 1920×1080, 60 fps, exactly 900 frames / 15 seconds.
- Anton display lettering converted to actual vector glyph outlines, supporting Inter 600 only for small useful copy. Anton OFL retained, no borrowed brand.
- Ink #101014; electric yellow #F6D548; ice blue #99CBF9; paper #F2F0E8. Clear flat roles; no glow, decorative grain or repeated circular portals.
- Primary register: graphic, physical, precise. Different curves for cut/slam, lateral travel and dimensional settling. Camera/projection only when depth matters.
- Full-frame typography → dense woven rectangular field → one monumental dimensional letter → decisive type resolution.
- No persistent numbered header, frame border, footer timetable, fake product UI, ribbons or résumé facts.
- Not designed as a seamless loop. The final statement gets an intentional readable landing.

## Implementation / review

Remotion is the only clock. Framer Motion's `motion.g` / `motion.div` and cubic-bezier functions consume numeric frame-derived values with entry animation disabled. Projected 3D is custom CPU/SVG geometry, not a claim of Three.js use. One timeline JSON drives audio accents and visual events. No paid generation/API required.

Review selected states first, then close strips at 100, 200, 375, 450, 575, 650 and 750. Check legibility at phone width, mask seams, model proportions, sparse/dense contrast and final reading time. Technical QA must test random/fresh seeks and decode the actual final encoding. Frame reviews and audio signal checks are not uninterrupted audiovisual listening.
